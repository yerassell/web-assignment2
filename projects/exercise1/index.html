About her

Audrey Hepburn was a British actress, fashion icon, and dedicated humanitarian who remains one of Hollywood's most revered legends. After surviving severe hardship and malnutrition in the Nazi-occupied Netherlands during World War II, she initially trained as a ballerina before transitioning into acting. She quickly rose to international stardom, winning an Academy Award for Roman Holiday (1953) and capturing hearts in timeless classics like Breakfast at Tiffany's (1961). Later in life, she largely stepped away from cinema to serve as a tireless UNICEF Goodwill Ambassador, utilizing her global fame to advocate for impoverished children until her death in 1993.
